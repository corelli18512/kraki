# Kraki for Mac 0.2.91

- Fixed agents losing access to Documents, Desktop and other protected folders ("Operation not permitted") after an app update. Kraki now restarts its background service when an update replaces it, so Full Disk Access keeps working without restarting Kraki yourself.
- Long chat histories fill the window without needing a scroll first, and live action cards refresh when their content changes.
- After first setup, Kraki offers to connect your phone right away.
