# Kraki for iPhone 0.1.37

- Follows your text size setting in more places. Session cards, permission and question cards (Approve / Deny), Settings, Devices and account usage now grow with a larger text size. At the default size nothing changes.
- Changing the text size while a chat is open now re-lays out the conversation right away. Before, bubbles mixed old and new sizes until you reopened the Session.
- The last-message preview on Session cards is slightly darker and easier to read.
