## Privacy and service reliability

- Added a direct Privacy Policy link in Settings → Privacy.
- Clarified the difference between optional performance diagnostics and basic daily account activity counts.
- Added a content-free foreground activity signal for service statistics. It records an account/date, not conversations, audio or files; background connections are not counted as foreground use.

Performance sharing remains optional in Settings → Privacy → Share Performance Data. The basic activity tally is separate, with 35-calendar-day server retention.

Privacy Policy: https://app.kraki.chat/privacy/
