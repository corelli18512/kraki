## What's new in Kraki 0.2.80

- **Forking is instant:** copying a conversation now takes a fraction of a second, even for very long ones, and opens straight into the copy with its full history, no "Starting session…" wait. Copies are numbered ("Fork 2 of …") so you can tell them apart.
- **Forking while the agent is working:** the copy ends with a clear "Stopped" note instead of a question that never gets answered.
- **Sending right after opening a long conversation:** your message shows up immediately instead of hiding under the message box until the reply arrives.
- **Voice input:** the first-use notice and settings now say plainly that recordings and corrections are handled by third-party cloud services.
- **Privacy:** Kraki no longer stores your GitHub token in its background service settings.
