# Kraki CLI 0.38.11

- Pi: when a session is stopped or replaced, its pending subagent wait timers are cancelled at once, so a stale timer can no longer act on the new session.
- Pi: compaction status from a stopped or replaced session no longer shows on the current one.
- Claude: resuming a session that had not finished starting now recovers correctly.
