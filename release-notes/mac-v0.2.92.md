# Kraki for Mac 0.2.92

- Simplified Chinese: Kraki now follows your system language, or choose one in Settings › General.
- Rename any of your devices from Devices; the new name shows on every device.
- The message box was reworked: typing a long draft no longer lags, one-line drafts no longer fade at the bottom, Chinese and other input methods clear and send correctly, Undo/Redo work, and focus stays where you expect. Pasting from spreadsheets works, and pasted images stay with their own session.
- The menu bar shows "Reconnecting" when Kraki is running but cannot reach the relay.
- "Connect your phone" during first setup now shows "Start Using Kraki" once your phone joins.
- Chat history loads over the direct connection to this Mac while the relay is unreachable.
- Faster reconnects after sleep or a network change.
- Kraki no longer restarts its background service on every launch when it runs from a second copy of the app.
- Sign-in errors and table labels are shown in your language; "Relaunch Now" explains when it cannot relaunch.
