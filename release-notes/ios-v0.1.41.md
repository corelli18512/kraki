# Kraki for iPhone 0.1.41

- Searching Sessions with a Chinese (or other composing) keyboard no longer filters by the letters you are still typing. Results update once characters are entered, and the search field keeps your input instead of being interrupted.
- The Refresh button for Accounts on the Devices page stays visible. While a recent refresh is cooling down it looks normal (tapping it does nothing yet). When nothing can refresh, for example while disconnected, it is dimmed but still visible.
