## Kraki CLI 0.39.0

- **Device trust**: the trust store's file locks can no longer be taken over while another process is writing them, and a dead recovery lock is cleared without touching a newer one. On Windows, concurrent writers (the daemon plus `kraki pair` / `kraki connect`) no longer fail with `EPERM`; a brief file-sharing conflict is retried.
- **Renamed apps** show their new name in the trusted-devices list; apps that were never renamed keep the name they paired with.
- **Security**: only Head-format sign-in challenges are signed; payloads reassembled from fragments are credited to their real sender; `wrong_region` redirects are followed only to trusted relays.
- **Connection**: a dead relay connection is dropped at once instead of waiting about 30 s for a close frame; reconnect catch-up asks only for what is missing.
- **Pi**: turns no longer stay "running" (and keep the Mac awake) because a subagent widget kept repainting; retries, stalls and compaction are logged.
- **Runtime**: stop signals are handled from the first moment; the daemon no longer restarts twice after a Mac update; logs rotate by age too; the web app's diagnostic logs are accepted again.
- Logs record lengths instead of titles and prompt text.
