## What's new in Kraki 0.2.77

- **Custom Words sync right away.** Words you add or remove on your phone or in the browser show up on this Mac immediately, and removed words no longer come back.
- **Sign Out works.** Kraki stays signed out until you sign in again; this Mac keeps serving its agents meanwhile.
- **After a restart** the menu bar shows whether this Mac is online, even before you open the window.
- **Updating** no longer asks whether to take this Mac offline.
- **Devices:** remove an old device in Settings › Devices, and see when an offline one was last online.
- **Offline computers:** the message box stays and your message is delivered when the computer is back.
- **Steps** show each permission once, with what happened: Approved, or Denied with your reason.
- **Sessions:** Rename… and Archive in the session menu; forks are named "Fork of …"; one name for each model everywhere.
- Settings follow Light/Dark, and say plainly when notifications are off or voice isn't available.
