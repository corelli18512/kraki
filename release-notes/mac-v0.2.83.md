## What's new in Kraki 0.2.83

- **Pair your devices with your computer:** scan the computer's QR code (or, on the same Mac, it pairs by itself) and your messages are signed and checked end to end, so the relay can't read or send commands as you. Each computer's settings show its safety code and trusted devices, and you can turn on "Only paired devices". Nothing changes until you do: unpaired apps keep working.
- **Help Improve Kraki:** Kraki now shares performance data, such as how quickly replies arrive. A one-time notice explains it; turn it off any time in Settings → General → Share Performance Data.
- **Hover text everywhere:** every icon-only button says what it does, and a greyed-out one says why.
- **Menus that work with the composer:** ⌘W, ⌘M and ⌘H are back; switching sessions is now ⌥⌘↑/⌥⌘↓ so ⌘↑/⌘↓ move in the message you're typing; the Session menu lists your session titles and has Rename and Archive.
- **Menu bar octopus:** a lighter icon, and a small blue dot when something needs you.
- **Approvals cover one request:** "always allow" is gone everywhere; each approval applies to that request only.
- **Fixes:** replies and questions no longer get stuck or misplaced after reconnects and steering; pasted text ending in a new line stays a draft; empty Steps keep their title at the top; tool arguments show readable values; Account Usage follows your dark/light theme; the working dots in the sidebar use the right blue in dark mode.
