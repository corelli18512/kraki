## What's new in Kraki 0.2.82

- **Pick up where you left off:** opening a conversation with new messages (a red dot, or from a notification) now starts at the first message you haven't read when the new part is longer than one screen. If it fits, you land at the bottom as before. It stays there while more arrives, until you scroll, tap ↓ or send.
- **Smoother replies:** while a reply streams, the conversation glides up line by line instead of jumping. A reply that arrives all at once slides in smoothly, with no jump.
