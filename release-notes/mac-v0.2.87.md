## Privacy and service reliability

- Added a public, bilingual Privacy Policy and a direct link in Privacy settings.
- Clarified that optional performance uploads and basic daily account activity counts are separate. Turning off Share Performance Data stops performance uploads and removes unsent performance data.
- Added a minimal foreground activity signal so service reports can count active accounts without counting background agents or collecting conversation content. The server keeps only an account/date record, with a 35-calendar-day retention window.

Privacy Policy: https://app.kraki.chat/privacy/
