# Kraki for iPhone 0.1.46

- **Save and share chat images**: open an image and tap **Save to Photos** or **Share…**, or long-press it in the chat. You get the original file, so a GIF stays an animated GIF; Share… also lets you copy it or send it to Messages and Files. The first time you save, Kraki asks for permission to add photos.
- **Animated GIFs play** in chats and in the viewer, and the viewer has Play/Pause. A GIF without a loop count loops forever. With Reduce Motion on, a GIF shows its first frame until you play it.
- **Sending GIFs**: a GIF you pick is sent as a GIF instead of being turned into a still picture. A GIF that is too large is made smaller and stays animated. While a photo is loading or being prepared, the image slot shows that it is loading and Send waits; × cancels it.
- Devices show a PC running Kraki for Windows by the app's name and version. Once it runs Kraki for Windows 0.1.12 or later, you can update it from your iPhone.
